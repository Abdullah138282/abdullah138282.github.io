Questions, bug reports, article corrections and privacy requests are all welcome.

You can also contact us at thinkora20@gmail.com . We welcome questions, feedback, corrections and website-related reports.

What to include in your message

  • Bug report: tell us the page, device and browser you used, along with what happened and what you expected.
  • Article correction: include the article link and explain which statement needs correction. A reliable source is helpful.
  • Privacy request: see our privacy policy for information about privacy requests and how to contact us about them.
