What is a good IQ score?

Updated October 1, 2026  ·  5 min read

Key takeaways

  • 100 is the average IQ score on the standard scale, while 90–109 is commonly treated as the average range.
  • Scores of 110, 120 and 130 are progressively less common and fall above the average.
  • IQ is only one measure of cognitive performance and should not be treated as a complete measure of a person.
  • Online IQ tests provide estimates, so results can differ from professionally administered assessments.

The short answer

A good IQ score generally means a score above the average of 100. On the commonly used IQ scale with a mean of 100 and a standard deviation of 15, scores around 110 are above average, 120 are clearly uncommon, and 130 are around the 98th percentile. However, there is no single IQ score that officially defines a “good” IQ for everyone.

What an IQ score means depends on the test used, its scoring system, and the purpose for which the result is being considered. A score should therefore be interpreted in context rather than treated as an exact measure of overall intelligence.

What is the average IQ?

The average IQ score is 100. Modern standardized IQ tests are designed around a statistical distribution in which the mean score is 100. On a scale with a standard deviation of 15, many people score relatively close to that average.

A score of 100 does not mean that someone has exactly the same cognitive abilities as everyone else. It simply represents the center of the reference distribution used by that particular test.

IQ score ranges

The following ranges provide a simple way to understand commonly reported IQ scores on a standard scale with a mean of 100 and standard deviation of 15.

Common IQ score ranges on a standard scale
IQ score General description Approximate percentile
Below 70 Very low range Below about 2nd
70–79 Low range About 2nd–8th
80–89 Below average About 9th–23rd
90–109 Average About 25th–73rd
110–119 Above average About 75th–90th
120–129 High range About 91st–97th
130+ Very high range About 98th+

These descriptions are general rather than universal. Different IQ tests and organizations may use different labels or boundaries, so the classification printed on a particular test report can vary.

Is 110 a good IQ score?

A score of 110 is above the average of 100. On a standard scale with a standard deviation of 15, it is around the 75th percentile, meaning the score is higher than roughly three quarters of the reference population.

Is 120 a good IQ score?

A score of 120 is well above average. On the same standard scale, it is around the 91st percentile. That means a score at this level is higher than the scores of roughly 9 out of 10 people in the reference population.

Is 130 a high IQ?

A score of 130 is substantially above average. On a standard scale with a mean of 100 and standard deviation of 15, it corresponds to approximately the 98th percentile.

Some organizations use a score around 130 as one criterion when considering giftedness or high cognitive ability, but there is no universal rule that makes 130 an official cutoff in every setting. Requirements and definitions can vary between organizations, assessments and purposes.

How rare is a high IQ score?

The higher the IQ score, the less common it becomes within the reference population. On a standard scale with a mean of 100 and standard deviation of 15, some approximate landmarks are:

Approximate IQ percentiles on a standard scale
IQ Approximate percentile Approximate frequency
110 75th About 1 in 4
120 91st About 1 in 11
130 98th About 1 in 44
140 99.6th About 1 in 261

These are theoretical estimates based on the statistical model of the scale. Actual test populations and individual test norms can differ.

Why IQ is not the whole picture

An IQ score measures performance on specific types of cognitive tasks. Depending on the assessment, these can include areas such as reasoning, working memory, processing speed, verbal abilities and visual or spatial reasoning.

IQ does not directly measure every ability that can matter in everyday life. Creativity, motivation, curiosity, persistence, communication, practical knowledge and social skills are not represented by a single IQ number.

What an IQ score does not tell you

An IQ score is not a complete description of a person’s abilities, potential or value. It is a standardized measurement of performance on particular cognitive tasks.

Why can IQ scores differ between tests?

The same person can receive different IQ scores from different tests. Assessments can use different questions, scoring procedures, reference groups and standardization samples. Performance can also be affected by factors such as familiarity with testing, attention, fatigue and the conditions in which the test is taken.

This is one reason an IQ score should not be interpreted as an perfectly precise number. Professional assessments also account for measurement error when interpreting results.

Online IQ tests vs. professional assessments

A free online IQ test can provide an informal estimate of performance, but it is not automatically equivalent to a professionally administered psychological assessment.

If an IQ result is needed for an important educational, clinical or professional decision, a qualified professional should conduct and interpret the appropriate assessment. For a casual estimate, you can try the free Thinkora IQ test.

How to understand your IQ score

The most useful way to read an IQ score is to consider its percentile, the test used to obtain it and the purpose of the assessment. Looking at a score in isolation can make small differences seem more important than they really are.

Frequently asked questions

What is considered a good IQ score?

There is no universal cutoff for a “good” IQ score. On the standard scale, 100 is average, while scores above 100 are above average. Scores around 120 or higher are relatively uncommon, and 130 is around the 98th percentile.

Is an IQ of 120 good?

An IQ of 120 is well above average and corresponds to approximately the 91st percentile on a standard scale with a mean of 100 and standard deviation of 15.

Is an IQ of 130 good?

An IQ of 130 is substantially above average and is approximately at the 98th percentile on the same standard scale. Some organizations use 130 as one criterion related to giftedness, but there is no universal cutoff for every purpose.

What is the average IQ?

The average IQ is 100 on the standard IQ scale. Many commonly used IQ tests are designed around a mean of 100 and a standard deviation of 15.

Does a high IQ guarantee success?

No. IQ measures specific cognitive abilities and does not capture every factor that can influence academic, professional or personal outcomes.
