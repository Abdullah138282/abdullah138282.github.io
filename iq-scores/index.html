IQ scores explained

A plain guide to IQ ranges, what each label means, and how rare each score really is.

Last updated October 1, 2026

What an IQ score tells you

An IQ score compares how you performed on a test with how a large reference group performed. It is a rank, not a measurement of how much intelligence you have. On the standard scale the average is 100 and the standard deviation is 15, so about 68 out of 100 people score between 85 and 115 and about 95 out of 100 score between 70 and 130. IQ score ranges and classifications

Psychologists group scores into bands. The names below follow the labels used for the Wechsler scales. The share column is the approximate part of the population in each band if scores follow a perfect bell curve.

How rare is each IQ score?

The higher or lower you go from 100, the fewer people you find. Each extra 15 points makes the group several times smaller, and the drop gets steeper as you climb.

Scores above about 145 are very hard to measure well. Few people sit in the sample used to build the test, so the numbers carry big error margins. Look up any score in the IQ percentile calculator.

Why the same person can get different scores

  • Different tests use different scales. Most use a standard deviation of 15, but some use 16 or 24, so the same rank gives a different number.
  • Measurement error. Even a professionally given test has a margin of a few points either way, so a score is better read as a range than a single number.
  • Test quality. A test normed on a small or unrepresentative sample gives less reliable results. Free online tests are estimates.
  • The day. Sleep, stress, distraction and practice effects all move the result a little.

What an IQ score does not tell you

  • How creative, kind, motivated or wise you are.
  • How well you will do in a particular job or subject.
  • Whether you have a learning difference or a medical condition. Only a qualified professional can assess that.

Questions about IQ scores

What is a normal IQ score?

Scores from 90 to 109 are considered average, and about half of all people fall in that range. Scores from 85 to 115 cover roughly two out of three people.

What is a good IQ score?

Anything at or above 110 is above average, and 120 or more is higher than about 91 out of 100 people. What counts as good depends on what you want to use the number for, and an IQ score is only one narrow measure of how people think.

What IQ score is considered genius?

There is no official cut-off. People often use 140 and above as an informal marker, which is roughly 1 in 260 people on the standard scale. Psychologists rarely use the word genius as a clinical label.

Is an IQ of 100 smart?

It is average by definition: tests are calibrated so that 100 is the median score. Half of people score above it and half below.

Does IQ change over time?

Scores are fairly stable from adulthood, but they can move a few points between tests and sessions because of normal measurement error, sleep, motivation and test familiarity.
