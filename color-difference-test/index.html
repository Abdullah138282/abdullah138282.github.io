How the color difference test works

Each round shows a grid of tiles in the same color, with one tile a little lighter or darker. Click the odd tile to score a point. The grid gets bigger every couple of rounds and the difference between the shades gets smaller. You have 60 seconds, and each wrong click takes 4 seconds off the clock. Your score is the number of odd tiles you found.

Tips for a higher score

  • Look at the whole board at once, not tile by tile. The odd one often stands out at a glance.
  • If you cannot see it after a second or two, click your best guess and move on. Wrong clicks cost time but dwelling costs more.
  • Turn your screen brightness up and avoid glare.

Common questions

Is this a color blindness test?

No. It is a game for fun and does not test or diagnose color vision. If you are worried about your eyesight, see an eye care professional.

Why do my scores change on different devices?

Screens show color differently, and brightness and viewing angle change how easy small shade differences are to see.
