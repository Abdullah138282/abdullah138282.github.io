About Thinkora

A free place to test your mind and understand your results.

What Thinkora is

Thinkora is a free website for people who are curious about how their mind works. You can take our online IQ test, play short brain games that measure things like reaction time and memory, and use calculators that turn a raw number into something you can understand.

Everything on the site is free and works without an account. You can start a test straight away and see your result when you finish.

What we offer

  • IQ test: an estimate of your score on the standard scale.
  • Brain games: reaction time, number memory, pattern memory, colour difference and mental maths.
  • IQ percentile calculator and the IQ score guide: tools to understand a score you already have.
  • Articles: plain-language explanations of the research behind IQ, memory and speed of thinking.

How we think about accuracy

Thinkora results are estimates. A reliable IQ score comes from a long test given one to one by a trained psychologist and compared with a large, carefully built sample of people. A free test on a website cannot do that, and we do not pretend it can. We say so on every test page, and we explain how scores are worked out on the methodology page.

That is why we describe results as estimated, show ranges and percentiles instead of single bold claims, and never present a score as a medical, educational or hiring decision tool.

How we write

Articles explain ideas in plain language and avoid hype. When we quote a figure, such as the share of people above a score, it comes from the normal distribution or from published research, and we correct mistakes when readers point them out. If you spot something wrong, tell us.

How the site is funded

Thinkora is free to use. The site may show advertising to cover hosting and development. Ads never change a test result, and they do not decide what we write. You can read more about cookies and ad partners in our privacy policy.
