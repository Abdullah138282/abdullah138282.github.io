About Thinkora

A free place to test your mind, play brain games and understand your results.

What is Thinkora?

Thinkora is a free website for people who are curious about how their mind works. You can take our online IQ test, play short brain games that measure skills such as reaction time and memory, and use IQ calculators and guides to understand your results.

Everything on Thinkora is designed to be simple to use. You can start a test or game without creating an account, entering an email address or subscribing to a service. Results are shown when you finish.

What Thinkora offers

  • Free IQ test: an online test that provides an estimated score on the standard IQ scale.
  • Free brain games: reaction time, number memory, pattern memory, colour difference and mental maths games.
  • IQ percentile calculator and the IQ score guide: tools for understanding IQ scores, ranges and percentiles.
  • Articles: plain-language explanations about IQ, memory, reaction speed and related topics.

How we approach accuracy

Thinkora test results are estimates. A professionally administered IQ assessment is typically conducted under controlled conditions and interpreted using standardized procedures. A free online test cannot reproduce every part of that process, so we do not present an online result as a clinical diagnosis or professionally administered IQ assessment.

We explain how our scoring works on the methodology page. We also use language such as estimated score, percentile and score range rather than presenting an online result as an exact measurement of a person's abilities.

Online test results can also vary because of differences in test design, questions, scoring methods, testing conditions and measurement error. For more context, see our article about whether online IQ tests are accurate.

How we write and explain information

Thinkora aims to explain IQ and cognitive testing topics in plain language without unnecessary hype. When we use figures about IQ distributions or percentiles, we explain the scale and assumptions behind them where relevant.

We also aim to correct factual errors when they are identified. If you notice something that needs correction or clarification, you can contact Thinkora.

How Thinkora is funded

Thinkora is free to use. The site may display advertising to help cover hosting, development and other operating costs. Advertising does not determine your test result.

Information about cookies, advertising and how information is handled is available in our privacy policy.

Our approach to online IQ tests

Thinkora is intended to provide a convenient way to explore IQ-style questions, brain games and related information online. A Thinkora score should be treated as an estimate rather than a replacement for a professionally administered assessment.
