What the IQ test measures

Intelligence is not one thing. The free IQ test looks at five kinds of reasoning, so your result shows where you are strongest.

Spatial reasoning
Picturing shapes, rotating them in your head and spotting how patterns fit together.
Numerical reasoning
Finding the rule behind number series and working with quantities under time pressure.
Logical reasoning
Following a chain of clues to a conclusion and noticing what does not belong.
Verbal reasoning
Understanding word meanings and the logic of statements, such as what must follow from what you are told.
Applied reasoning
Using what you know to solve everyday problems you have not seen before.

An estimate, not a diagnosis

Thinkora gives you a good estimate of how you reason compared with other people. It is not a clinical test. Sleep, stress and distractions can move your score by several points, so treat it as a starting point, and see a licensed psychologist if you need an official assessment.

What Is an Online IQ Test?

An online IQ test uses questions and puzzles to estimate how you perform on different types of reasoning tasks. Learn more about online IQ test accuracy, how IQ scores are calculated, and what different IQ score ranges mean.

Common questions

Is the Thinkora IQ test free?

Yes. Your IQ estimate, percentile and skill breakdown are free. You do not need an account or an email address.

How accurate is an online IQ test?

Online tests give an estimate, not a diagnosis. Expect a margin of several points either way, and it can shift with sleep, focus and distractions. For a formal result, see a licensed psychologist.

What is a good IQ score?

Scores are set so the average is 100. About two thirds of people score between 85 and 115. A score above 130 is higher than roughly 98 percent of people. See our IQ score guide for more detail.

What does the IQ percentile mean?

A percentile shows the share of people you scored higher than. At the 84th percentile, you scored higher than about 84 out of 100 people.
