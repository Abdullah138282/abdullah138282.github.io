How the mental math test works

You get 60 seconds. Each problem is addition, subtraction or multiplication, and you type the answer and press Enter to move to the next one. Every five correct answers the numbers get larger, and multiplication joins in after ten. A wrong answer does not cost time but it breaks your streak. Your score is the number of correct answers.

Tips to go faster

  • Round numbers first. To add 49, add 50 and take away 1.
  • Learn the multiplication tables up to 12. They come up a lot.
  • Do not stop on a hard one. Enter your best answer and move on.
  • Use the number keys and Enter so you never need to touch the mouse.

Common questions

Can I use a calculator?

No. The point is to do the sums in your head.

What is a good score?

Scores depend a lot on age, practice and typing speed, so it is most useful to compare with your own earlier tries.

Is my score saved?

Your best score is kept on the page for this visit only.
