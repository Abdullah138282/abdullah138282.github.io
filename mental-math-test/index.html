How the mental math test works

This free mental math test gives you 60 seconds to solve arithmetic problems without using a calculator. You type the answer to each question and press Enter to move to the next one. The test includes addition, subtraction and multiplication.

The problems become more challenging as your score increases. Every five correct answers the numbers get larger, and multiplication is introduced after ten correct answers. Your final score is the number of correct answers you give during the 60-second challenge.

What does a mental math test measure?

A timed mental math game combines basic arithmetic with attention, recall of familiar number facts and speed of responding. Your result can also be affected by typing speed, familiarity with mental arithmetic and how comfortable you are working under a time limit.

This is an online brain game rather than a standardized assessment of mathematical ability. A single game score should not be treated as a formal measure of intelligence or academic performance.

Tips to solve mental math problems faster

  • Learn common multiplication facts so you can recall them without calculating from scratch.
  • Break larger numbers into easier parts. For example, 49 + 36 can be thought of as 50 + 36 − 1.
  • Keep track of the numbers carefully instead of rushing into an answer.
  • Use the number keys and Enter so you can answer without moving between the keyboard and mouse.
  • Practice regularly if you want to become more familiar with quick arithmetic.

What is a good mental math score?

There is no universal score that defines good mental math performance in this game. Results can vary with arithmetic experience, practice, typing speed and familiarity with the format.

For that reason, your own previous attempts can provide a more useful reference than comparing one game result with someone else's score.

Mental math test vs IQ test

Mental arithmetic is one type of cognitive task, while an IQ test typically includes several types of reasoning. A mental math game therefore should not be used as a replacement for an IQ assessment.

If you want to try a broader reasoning challenge, you can take the free online IQ test. You can also explore the other Thinkora brain games.

Common questions

Can I use a calculator?

No. The challenge is designed for mental arithmetic, so calculators are not part of the test.

What is a good score?

There is no single score that everyone should aim for. Results depend on arithmetic experience, practice and typing speed, so comparing your own attempts can be useful.

Does a wrong answer cost time?

No. A wrong answer does not directly reduce the timer, but it does not count toward your correct-answer score.

Is my score saved?

Your best score is kept on the page for the current visit only. It is not saved as a permanent account score.
