Privacy Policy

How Thinkora collects, uses and protects information when you use our website and services.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Thinkora. This Privacy Policy explains how Thinkora handles information when you visit or use https://abdullah138282.github.io/ and related pages, including our IQ tests, brain games, calculators, articles and contact features.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to operate the website, respond to users and improve our services.

This policy is intended to provide clear information for visitors worldwide, including visitors in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area and other jurisdictions where applicable.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

If you contact Thinkora through our contact form, we may receive:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The topic of your message
  • The message or information you choose to provide

You are not required to provide unnecessary personal information in a contact message.

Information generated when you use the website

Like most websites, basic technical information may be processed by our hosting, security or infrastructure providers when you access the website. Depending on the services involved, this may include information such as an IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, referring page, requested pages and approximate timestamps.

Thinkora does not intentionally ask visitors to provide sensitive personal information through its ordinary website features.

3. IQ Tests, Games and Calculators

Thinkora provides interactive tests, games and calculators for informational and entertainment purposes.

Where a feature allows you to enter answers, scores or other information, that information may be processed by the webpage in order to calculate or display your result.

Unless a feature specifically states otherwise, Thinkora does not require you to create an account to use these features.

Thinkora's estimated IQ results are not clinically validated and should not be used as a medical, educational, employment or professional diagnosis or decision-making tool.

4. Contact Form and Web3Forms

Our contact form uses Web3Forms to transmit messages submitted through the website.

When you submit the contact form, the information you provide may be processed by Web3Forms and transmitted to the email address configured for Thinkora's contact service.

The information submitted through the form may include your name, email address, selected topic and message.

We use this information to respond to questions, investigate bug reports, consider article corrections, handle privacy requests and respond to legitimate business inquiries.

We do not ask you to submit passwords, payment card information, government identification numbers, medical records or other highly sensitive information through our ordinary contact form.

5. How We Use Information

Depending on the circumstances, information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain Thinkora.
  • Respond to contact requests.
  • Investigate technical problems and bug reports.
  • Review corrections or feedback about our content.
  • Respond to privacy requests.
  • Protect the website against abuse, fraud and security threats.
  • Understand and improve website performance and user experience where appropriate.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Legal Bases for Processing

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, the legal basis for processing depends on the particular activity.

Depending on the circumstances, processing may be based on:

  • Your consent, where consent is required.
  • The need to respond to a request you make.
  • Our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving the website, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Thinkora may use browser storage technologies required for website functionality.

For example, our current theme selector may use browser local storage to remember whether you selected light or dark mode.

This information is used to remember your display preference and is not intended to identify you personally.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, personalization, or other technologies that use cookies or similar technologies in the future, this policy will be updated and any legally required choices or notices will be provided.

8. Third-Party Services

Thinkora may use third-party providers to operate parts of the website.

Our current contact form uses Web3Forms for form submission and email delivery.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual arrangements. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

We may add other third-party services in the future, such as analytics, advertising, security, hosting or performance services. When this happens, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe material changes.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to resolve requests, maintain appropriate records, protect the website, or comply with legal obligations.

Contact messages may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request and maintain legitimate business or legal records.

Retention periods may also depend on the retention practices of third-party service providers used to process submitted information.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. International Data Transfers

Thinkora may be operated from outside the country in which you live, and third-party service providers may process information in other countries.

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers of personal data, we intend to use appropriate mechanisms or safeguards required by that law.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and which privacy law applies, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

These may include rights to:

  • Know whether personal information about you is being processed.
  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to applicable exceptions.
  • Request restriction of processing in circumstances provided by applicable law.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Request portability of certain information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Opt out of certain forms of sale, sharing, targeted advertising or profiling where applicable law provides such rights.

The exact rights available to you depend on your location, the applicable law and the nature of the processing.

13. European Privacy Rights

If the GDPR or another applicable European data protection law applies to you, you may have additional rights, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, subject to applicable conditions and exceptions.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in the country where you live, work or where you believe a data protection infringement occurred.

14. United States Privacy Rights

Certain U.S. states provide residents with specific privacy rights. These rights and the obligations of a website depend on whether the relevant state law applies to the organization and its activities.

Where applicable law provides you with rights such as access, correction, deletion, portability or an opt-out from certain processing, you may submit a request using the contact information below.

Thinkora does not currently state that every U.S. state privacy law applies to it. Applicability depends on the requirements and thresholds of the relevant law.

15. California Residents

California residents may have privacy rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act and related California privacy legislation when those laws apply to the business and processing involved.

Depending on the circumstances, these rights may include the right to know, access, correct and delete certain personal information, as well as certain rights concerning sale, sharing and targeted advertising.

Thinkora does not sell personal information for money.

If our activities become subject to additional California requirements, we will provide the notices and choices required by applicable law.

16. Children's Privacy

Thinkora's services are not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes that require parental consent under applicable children's privacy laws.

We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children through our ordinary website features.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information to Thinkora that should not have been collected, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action where required by law.

17. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Thinkora does not currently sell personal information for monetary consideration.

If future services involve processing that legally constitutes a sale or sharing of personal information, we will provide the disclosures and opt-out mechanisms required by applicable law.

18. Third-Party Links

Thinkora may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website before providing personal information.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, technologies or legal obligations change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
