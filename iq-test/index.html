How the Thinkora IQ test works

This free IQ test has 30 questions divided into four reasoning areas: spatial reasoning (shapes and patterns), numerical reasoning (number series and word problems), logical reasoning (clues and deduction), and applied reasoning (everyday problem solving). The questions begin easier and become more challenging as the test continues. Harder questions count for more points. Learn more about IQ scores and how they are commonly interpreted, or read how IQ scores are calculated to understand how raw test performance is converted into a standardized score.

When you complete the online IQ test, your score is calculated in your browser and displayed immediately. The result uses a standard IQ scale where the average is 100 and about two thirds of people score between 85 and 115. Your result also includes an estimated percentile and a breakdown of your performance across the four reasoning areas. You can use our IQ percentile calculator to explore how different IQ scores compare with the wider population.

What your IQ score means

IQ scores are commonly described using ranges around an average of 100. The percentile gives another way to understand a score by showing approximately how many people would score lower on the same standardized scale. Tips for a fair IQ test result

  • Take the test when you are rested and will not be interrupted.
  • Read each question fully before you look at the answers.
  • If a question takes more than a minute, skip it and return later.
  • Use the answer keys 1 to 4 and the arrow keys to move faster on a computer.
  • Treat the result as a snapshot. Sleep, stress and mood can affect performance by several points.

Common questions about the IQ test

How long does the Thinkora IQ test take?

The Thinkora IQ test has 30 questions and a 20-minute time limit. Most people finish in about 15 to 20 minutes.

Is the Thinkora online IQ test accurate? Thinkora provides an estimated IQ score for information and entertainment, not a clinical diagnosis. Online IQ tests can differ from professionally administered intelligence assessments. Read our guide to online IQ test accuracy to understand what can affect online results.
Can I go back to a question on the IQ test?

Yes. Use the Back button to return to earlier questions and change your answers before you finish the test.

Does guessing help on an IQ test?

Wrong answers lose a small number of points, so random guessing does not raise your score. If you can rule out one or two options, an informed guess is reasonable. Otherwise, you can skip the question.

What is an average IQ score?

IQ scores are commonly standardized so that the average is 100, with about two thirds of people scoring between 85 and 115.
