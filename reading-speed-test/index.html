    How the reading speed test works

    You are shown one short passage of about 225 to 260 words and the timer starts as soon as it appears. When you press the finished button, the timer stops and the passage disappears. You then answer three questions about what you read.

    Your speed is the number of words in the passage divided by your reading time in minutes. Your effective speed multiplies that by the share of questions you answered correctly, so reading fast without taking anything in does not give you a high score. The test has three different passages, so you can try it more than once.

    What is a good reading speed?

    Studies of adults reading non-fiction silently in English find an average of roughly 240 words per minute, with fiction a little faster. Most healthy readers land somewhere between about 200 and 300. Speeds depend heavily on the text: dense technical material is read more slowly than a light story, and reading in a second language is usually slower than in your first.

    Speed versus understanding

    Reading faster is only useful if you still understand the text. Research on reading finds a trade-off between the two: when people push far beyond their normal pace, comprehension drops. That is why this test asks questions after the passage and reports an effective speed, instead of showing words per minute alone.

    Ways to read faster without losing understanding

    • Read more often. Familiar words and sentence patterns are recognized faster.
    • Build your vocabulary. Stopping on unknown words is one of the biggest slowdowns.
    • Avoid saying every word in your head or moving your lips. Try to take in short phrases at a time.
    • Do not re-read by habit. Keep moving forward and go back only when something does not make sense.
    • Remove distractions. A quiet screen with no notifications makes a visible difference.

    Reading speed and IQ

    Reading speed is not an intelligence measure, and a slower reader is not less intelligent. It reflects practice, language experience and attention. If you want a test of reasoning, try our free online IQ test, or sharpen your word knowledge with the vocabulary test.

    Common questions

    How is my reading speed calculated?

    The test counts the words in the passage and divides them by the time between pressing Start and pressing the finished button. The result is shown in words per minute (WPM).

    What is a normal reading speed?

    Research on adults reading non-fiction in English finds an average of roughly 240 words per minute for silent reading. Many healthy readers fall anywhere from about 200 to 300.

    Why are there comprehension questions?

    Speed alone can be misleading, because anyone can run their eyes over a page quickly without taking anything in. The three questions check that you understood what you read, and your effective speed adjusts your WPM by how many you got right.

    Can I read faster by speed reading?

    Most reading-speed research finds a trade-off between speed and understanding. Practice, a larger vocabulary and reading more often can raise your comfortable speed, but claims of very high speeds with full comprehension are not supported by good evidence.

    Is my result saved?

    No. Your result is shown at the end of the round and is not stored or sent anywhere.
