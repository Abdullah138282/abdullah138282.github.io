How the IQ percentile calculator works

An IQ percentile calculator shows where an IQ score falls compared with a reference population. IQ scores are commonly standardized around a mean of 100. On the standard scale, a standard deviation of 15 describes how widely scores are spread around that mean.

To estimate a percentile, the calculator first converts the IQ score into a z-score. It then uses the normal distribution to estimate the proportion of the reference population scoring below that point.

For example, an IQ score of 115 is one standard deviation above the mean on a scale with a mean of 100 and SD 15. That corresponds to approximately the 84th percentile.

How to read an IQ percentile

An IQ percentile represents relative rank, not a percentage of questions answered correctly. If your result is at the 90th percentile, your score is higher than approximately 90% of the comparison population.

For example, an IQ of 100 is at the 50th percentile on the standard scale. An IQ of 115 is around the 84th percentile, while an IQ of 130 is around the 97.7th percentile.

This means percentile differences become more noticeable at the higher end of the scale. A small change in IQ score can represent a larger change in relative rank when scores are near the extremes of the distribution.

IQ score vs IQ percentile

An IQ score and an IQ percentile describe related but different things. The IQ score is a standardized number, while the percentile describes where that number falls relative to a reference population.

For example, an IQ of 120 does not mean that a person answered 120% of questions correctly. On the standard scale, it corresponds to approximately the 90.9th percentile.

IQ percentile and standard deviation

Standard deviation is important when converting an IQ score to a percentile. The standard IQ scale commonly uses a mean of 100 and an SD of 15, but other IQ scales can use different standard deviations.

Thinkora's calculator includes SD 15, 16 and 24 so you can select the scale that matches the result you are trying to interpret. Because a different standard deviation changes the spread of the distribution, the same numerical IQ can produce a different percentile on another scale.

What percentile is an IQ of 100?

On the standard IQ scale, an IQ of 100 corresponds to the 50th percentile. The mean is set at 100, so a score of 100 represents the midpoint of the standard distribution.

What percentile is an IQ of 115?

On a scale with a mean of 100 and SD 15, an IQ of 115 is approximately the 84th percentile. It is one standard deviation above the mean.

What percentile is an IQ of 120?

On the standard scale, an IQ of 120 is approximately the 90.9th percentile. This means the score is higher than roughly 91% of the reference population.

What percentile is an IQ of 130?

An IQ of 130 on the standard scale is approximately the 97.7th percentile. In other words, the score is higher than approximately 97.7% of the comparison distribution.

IQ percentile calculator for online test results

You can use this IQ percentile calculator after receiving an IQ result from an online test. The calculator can show the approximate percentile associated with the numerical score when the same scale and standard deviation are used.

However, the percentile calculation itself does not make an online IQ test clinically validated. Online tests can use different questions, norms, scoring methods and testing conditions, so an online IQ result should generally be treated as an estimate.

IQ percentile and score rarity

Percentiles can also help describe how common or uncommon a score is within the reference distribution. For example, an IQ of 130 is around the 97.7th percentile on the standard scale, meaning approximately 2.3% of the distribution is above that point.

These figures describe a statistical distribution rather than a guarantee about a particular real-world population. The relevant reference group and test norms matter when interpreting an actual IQ score.

Why different IQ tests can give different percentiles

Different IQ tests may use different norm groups, scoring systems and standard deviations. Even when two tests report a number called an IQ score, their scales and reference data may not be identical.

This is why it is important to know the test and scale behind an IQ result before converting it to a percentile. When the scale is unknown, a percentile calculator can only provide an estimate based on an assumed distribution.

Questions about IQ percentiles

What does an IQ percentile mean?

An IQ percentile shows the percentage of people in the comparison population who score below a particular IQ score. For example, the 90th percentile means a score is higher than approximately 90 percent of the comparison population.

What percentile is an IQ of 130?

On an IQ scale with a mean of 100 and standard deviation of 15, an IQ of 130 is approximately at the 97.7th percentile.

What IQ is the 50th percentile?

An IQ of 100 corresponds to the 50th percentile on the standard IQ scale with a mean of 100.

What is the difference between IQ score and IQ percentile?

An IQ score is a standardized numerical score, while an IQ percentile describes how that score compares with scores in a reference population.

Why can the same IQ score have different percentiles?

The percentile depends on the IQ scale and its standard deviation. A scale using a standard deviation of 15 can produce a different percentile from a scale using another standard deviation for the same numerical IQ.

Can I use an IQ percentile calculator for an online IQ test?

Yes. An IQ percentile calculator can provide a rough comparison for an online IQ test result, but online IQ scores should generally be treated as estimates rather than clinical assessments.
